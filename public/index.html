Known limits
  • Ratings are held fixed for the rest of the season. A team does not get stronger inside a simulation by winning simulated matches.
  • Simulated matches draw a scoreline from real results, conditioned on the win/draw/loss outcome and the pre-match rating gap, so goal difference moves within a simulation and ties break on simulated goal difference — not today's. The rating-implied probabilities still decide who wins.
  • Each close season every rating mean-reverts 5% toward the pool average, so one freak year does not carry. Starting ratings still come only from where each club finished in 2025; transfers, injuries and European commitments are not modelled.
  • Two OBOS-ligaen clubs were promoted from the third tier, which is outside this project's data. They start at the bottom of the ladder and are corrected only by results.
  • Season shape is sampled by date, not by round. Rounds run out of order — Eliteserien 2026 plays round 12 after rounds 13 to 16 — so a round axis would show results before they happened.
