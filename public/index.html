How it works
  1. 01Rating

    Each club has a strength rating that moves after every match. Expected goals (xG) separate luck from good play.

  2. 02Goals

    A separate goals model counts goals scored and conceded. The match odds blend the two.

  3. 03Simulation

    The rest of the season is played 50,000 times. The table chances are how often each club finishes in each place.
